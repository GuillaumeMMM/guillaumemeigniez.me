Between 2019 and 2023 I worked at [Manadge](https://manadge.com), a Parisian startup building a B2B SaaS platform for aggregating, analyzing and visualizing programmatic advertising data.

I was hired as the first employee, to develop the Angular web app. Over the years I built the app itself with its modular interactive dashboards, its user management, and all its custom elements, such as a search and a range picker. I also developed a standalone styles and components library as well as a back office app.

I had the opportunity to mentor another frontend developer for 2 years, who went from junior to mid-level.

After I'd spent 4 years at Manadge, the company was acquired by iHeartMedia and the platform is still running today.
