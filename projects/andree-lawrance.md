Since I bought a sewing machine in 2017, I have gathered some of my sewing creations under the name André·e Lawrance.

I make sewing patterns for jackets, shorts, pants and bags. I buy fabric, sew the clothes, then go on trips to take pictures of them. Sometimes on my own, and sometimes with friends.
